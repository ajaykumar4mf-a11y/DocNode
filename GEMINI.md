# Human-Style Copywriting & UI Design Guidelines

When updating code, UI components, headings, button labels, or microcopy:
1. **Human-Centric Copy**: Write copy naturally, cleanly, and authentically—as if crafted by an experienced human developer/designer.
2. **Avoid AI Clichés**: Do NOT insert artificial AI tropes, decorative sparkler emojis (e.g. `✨`), floating pill badges (e.g. `#1 Rated Platform`, `Categories`), or over-the-top corporate buzzwords unless explicitly requested by the user.
3. **Clean & Grounded Styling**: Keep UI layouts, micro-interactions, and visual hierarchy clean, elegant, and grounded in standard web design best practices.
